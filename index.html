WHO I AM / WHAT DRIVES MY WORK

I turn spatial ideas into
experiences worth remembering.

I'm Yogesh Vikhankar, a 3D artist and spatial visualizer specializing in exhibition environments, corporate event spaces, stage concepts and immersive branded experiences. I develop environments that help ideas become tangible — long before they are built.

My workflow connects spatial planning, Autodesk Maya modeling, material development and Unreal Engine lighting to purposeful composition. From small exhibits to multi-zone event worlds, every image is designed to communicate scale, mood and intent.

Every frame should communicate an idea. Every space should leave an impression.
